Privacy Notice — Apollo Hódító értesítő

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Purpose

Apollo Hódító értesítő is a personal-use notification utility operated by its owner. It is intended to help the owner receive selected Gmail-related notifications.

Information accessed and used

When authorized by its owner, the application accesses Gmail data within the permissions granted through Google OAuth. The application uses that access to identify relevant messages and prepare notifications. It is not intended to provide Gmail access to other users.

Storage and sharing

Application processing is performed for the owner's personal use. Depending on the configuration, information needed to produce a notification may be processed on the owner's equipment and sent to the messaging service configured by the owner. That service handles data under its own terms and privacy practices.

Third-party services

Google provides account authorization and Gmail access. A configured messaging provider may deliver notifications. Those providers' own privacy notices apply to their services.

Data retention and control

The owner controls the application's configuration and can revoke its Google account access through their Google Account security settings. Operational records, if any, are managed by the owner as part of running the utility.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator using the support contact displayed on the Google consent screen.
